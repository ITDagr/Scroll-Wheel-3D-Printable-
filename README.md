3D Printed Scroll Wheel-
A diametric magnet endless scroll wheel based on the Adafruit ESP32 Feather with 4 PIN JST AS5600 Magnetic Sensor and 400 MAH Battery
Parts List:
[ESP32 S3 Feather ](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5477)
[AS5600 Angle Sensor](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6357)
[400 MAH LIPO battery (Small, low battery life),(Gen 2 will have a larger battery)](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3898)
[6810 Bearing](https://www.amazon.com/uxcell-6810-2RS-Groove-Bearings-Double/dp/B082PXZLJT/ref=sr_1_3?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.RiYwW4IISLAHUFuJjhncGaL5TM6_qp0PT8I_Hk7021IV6fJmnxixDLi6WxamUEbSt7PPm1bi8r32q_EUXm06HrQ71vLYtx4u9izhylfYnL58K_oVoKm8R4pzTNIk9vAKE_HwvewKh6lhMZJIxDgTEL_4GKpc7mKw-Ox3jaPIbKkFRJGHv9y2_hhtrRGzzWVnnB4Nifg68PaoK9z-a9Laaqaqy-hyGXgw5z5AkKTIoWE.ZQe6s7zVtfW7PXobO5uTGSENFlTjQc1f0spS6JyS21I&dib_tag=se&keywords=6810%2Bbearing&qid=1794730904&sr=8-3&th=1)
[6x 2.5MM Radial Flux Magnet]((https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/radial-magnets-inc/9042/5640338?s=N4IgTCBcDaIE4EMAmBLBAbABAWwQcwDsBTAFwGcQAaEATgAYAWCAXQF8g)), Radial flux is mandatory due to its special field properties being used to determind the precise angle when turning. 
Every other part is completely 3d printable in a total of 3 pieces.

This scroll wheel is based off of Engineer Bo's Wireless High Resolution Scrolling wheel that I found was inaccessible to anyone that lacked a soldering iron or the ability to custom order the PCB. My version, although less polished, is full accessible for modification to those who are complete beginners. I hope you can appreciate this iteration and find other purposes for it. Im currently iterating a version 2 with LED's, a custom PCB, and buttons. I might include some more if I can. I also have another iteration almost complete based on the [Smart Knob View by Scott Bez](url). It is a more minimalist concept with some really interesting gimmicks that i will release soon.
