3D Printed Scroll Wheel-
A diametric magnet endless scroll wheel based on the Adafruit ESP32 Feather with 4 PIN JST AS5600 Magnetic Sensor and 400 MAH Battery
Parts List:
[ESP32 S3 Feather ](url)
[AS5600 Angle Sensor](url)
[400 MAH LIPO battery (Small, low battery life),(Gen 2 will have a larger battery)](url)
[6810 Bearing](url)
This scroll wheel is based off of Engineer Bo's Wireless High Resolution Scrolling wheel that i found to be inaccessible to anyone that lacked a soldering iron or the ability to custom order the PCB. My version, although less polished, is full accessible for modification to those who are complete beginners. I hope you can appreciate this iteration and find other purposes for it. Im currently iterating a version 2 with LED's, a custom PCB, and buttons. I might include some more if I can. I also have another iteration almost complete based on the [Smart Knob View by Scott Bez](url). It is a more minimalist concept that i will release soon.
